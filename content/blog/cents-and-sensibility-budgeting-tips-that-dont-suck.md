Budgeting has a reputation problem. It sounds like punishment. It does not have to be.

## Start with awareness, not restriction

Track your spending for one month before changing anything. You cannot fix what you have not measured.

## The 50/30/20 shortcut

- 50% needs
- 30% wants
- 20% savings and debt payoff

## Automate the boring parts

Set up automatic transfers to savings the day you get paid. What you do not see, you will not spend.
