Decluttering advice can feel like a personality test you are constantly failing. Here is a kinder version.

## Start with the easy wins

Expired items, broken things, and duplicates you forgot you owned. These decisions require zero emotional energy.

## The real question to ask

Not "does this spark joy," but "have I used this in the last year, and will I realistically use it next year?"

## Give yourself permission

You do not need to declutter your whole house in a weekend. One drawer done well beats one closet abandoned halfway through.
