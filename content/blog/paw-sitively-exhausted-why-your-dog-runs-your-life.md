Somewhere between adoption day and now, the balance of power in your house shifted permanently.

## Signs your dog is in charge

1. You wake up at 6am, not because you want to, but because someone is staring at you
2. Your schedule bends around walk times
3. The good side of the couch has always been theirs

## Why we let it happen

Unconditional loyalty is a hard thing to say no to. Somehow a wagging tail beats every argument you had prepared.

## The real conclusion

You didn't get a pet. The pet got staff.
