Jet lag does not care about your itinerary. Here is how to fight back.

## Before you fly

Start shifting your sleep schedule two or three days ahead of departure, even by 30 minutes a day.

## During the flight

- Set your watch to the destination time zone immediately
- Avoid alcohol, it makes dehydration and grogginess worse
- Sleep only if it lines up with nighttime at your destination

## After you land

Get outside. Sunlight is the fastest way to reset your internal clock, faster than any pill or app claims to.
