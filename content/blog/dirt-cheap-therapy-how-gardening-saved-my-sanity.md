There is something deeply calming about getting your hands dirty on purpose.

## Why gardening works as therapy

Repetitive, physical tasks like weeding or transplanting give your mind something simple to focus on, which quiets the noise everywhere else.

## Start small

- One raised bed or even a few pots on a balcony
- Herbs are forgiving and useful almost immediately
- Expect to kill something in year one, everyone does

## The payoff

Watching something grow because of your effort is a quiet kind of proof that things can get better with consistent care.
