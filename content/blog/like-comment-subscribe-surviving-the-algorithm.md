Every platform wants one thing: more of your time. Understanding that changes how you use them.

## What the algorithm actually optimizes for

Engagement, not happiness. Outrage and anxiety keep you scrolling just as effectively as joy does, sometimes more so.

## Taking back some control

- Curate who you follow aggressively, unfollow anyone who drains you
- Use time limits, even if you ignore them half the time
- Remember that most feeds are a highlight reel, not a documentary

## The bigger picture

You are allowed to opt out of things designed to be addictive. That is not falling behind, that is just noticing the design.
