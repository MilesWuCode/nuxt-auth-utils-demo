Every home cook has a graveyard of failed recipes. This is a tribute to them.

## The greatest hits of kitchen chaos

- The soufflé that collapsed the second the oven door opened
- Rice that somehow burned on the bottom and stayed raw on top
- The "quick" 20-minute recipe that took two hours and three dishes you don't own

## Why it still matters

Every burnt pan is a lesson. You learn heat control, timing, and patience the hard way, one smoke alarm at a time.

## A tip that actually helps

Read the entire recipe before you start cooking. Not the first three lines. All of it. Future you will thank present you.
