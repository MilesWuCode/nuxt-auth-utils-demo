Talking about the weather gets mocked as boring small talk, but it is actually a finely tuned social skill.

## Why it works so well

Weather is universal, low-stakes, and gives both people an easy way in without revealing anything personal.

## Leveling up your weather talk

- Add a light observation: "This is early for it to be this warm"
- Connect it to plans: "Hope it clears up for the weekend"
- Know when to let the topic go and move to something real

## The hidden purpose

It was never about the weather. It is a warm-up lap before the actual conversation begins.
