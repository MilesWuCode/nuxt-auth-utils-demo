Gyms are packed in January and empty by February. The problem usually isn't willpower, it's the goal itself.

## Why big resolutions collapse

Vague, all-or-nothing goals like "get fit" or "save money" give you no clear next action, so motivation alone has to carry the entire plan.

## What works better

- Pick one specific, small habit instead of an entire lifestyle overhaul
- Track the habit, not just the outcome
- Expect to miss a day occasionally, and restart without the guilt spiral

## The real secret

Consistency over months beats intensity over weeks. Nobody regrets the boring habit that actually stuck.
