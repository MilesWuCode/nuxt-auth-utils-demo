If you have ever bought a plant with good intentions and buried it three weeks later, this one is for you.

## The number one killer

It is not neglect. It is overwatering. Most houseplants would rather be forgotten for a week than drowned every day out of love.

## Pick plants that forgive you

- Pothos: nearly impossible to kill
- Snake plant: thrives on being ignored
- ZZ plant: survives office lighting and your questionable memory

## A simple rule

Stick a finger into the soil. If it is dry two knuckles down, water it. If it is still damp, walk away and live your life.
