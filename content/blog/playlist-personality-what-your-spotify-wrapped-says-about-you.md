Every December, everyone becomes a music critic overnight, armed with a single chart from an app.

## What your top genre might reveal

- Sad indie folk: you have feelings and a cardigan
- High-energy pop: you are either always at the gym or wish you were
- Podcasts as your top category: you have simply stopped listening to music

## Is any of it real?

Not scientifically, no. But it is a fun mirror, and mirrors do not need to be accurate to be entertaining.

## The real lesson

Your taste evolving is normal. The playlist you are embarrassed by from three years ago was still valid at the time.
