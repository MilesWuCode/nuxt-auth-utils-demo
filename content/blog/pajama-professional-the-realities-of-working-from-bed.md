Working from bed sounds like the dream until your back files a formal complaint.

## The appeal is obvious

No commute, maximum comfort, and the illusion that you are still resting while technically working.

## The hidden cost

Poor posture, blurred boundaries between rest and work, and a strange feeling that you never truly left the office because you never truly left the bed.

## A better compromise

Keep a dedicated small workspace, even a corner of a room. Your brain needs a physical cue that work has started and, more importantly, that it has ended.
