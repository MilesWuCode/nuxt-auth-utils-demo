Your phone is not neutral. It was built by very smart people to keep you looking at it.

## Small changes, real results

- Turn off all non-essential notifications
- Move social apps off your home screen
- Switch your phone to grayscale for a week

## The one-hour rule

Give yourself one hour after waking up and one hour before bed with no screens. It feels impossible for about three days, then it feels like freedom.

## Why it's worth it

Attention is the scarcest resource you have. Everything else, you can get back.
