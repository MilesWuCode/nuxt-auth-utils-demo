Coffee is not a beverage. It is a personality trait, a morning ritual, and for some of us, a legally acceptable substitute for a functioning nervous system.

## Why we love it

There is something about the smell of fresh grounds that no alarm clock can replicate. Coffee wakes you up before the caffeine even hits your bloodstream.

## Brewing better at home

- Use freshly ground beans, not pre-ground dust that has been sitting in a bag since last winter
- Water temperature matters: aim for 90-96°C, not a rolling boil
- Invest in a cheap scale. Eyeballing ratios is how you end up with battery acid

## The bottom line

You do not need a $3,000 espresso machine to enjoy a great cup. You need good beans, clean water, and five extra minutes you were going to spend scrolling anyway.
